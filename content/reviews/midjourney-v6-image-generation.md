## What is Midjourney?

Midjourney is a leading AI image generation tool that creates stunning, photorealistic artwork from text prompts. Version 6 brings significant improvements in quality and control.

## Key Features

- **Photorealistic Quality**: Industry-leading image quality
- **Multiple Styles**: From photorealistic to artistic
- **Pan & Zoom**: Detailed editing with precise control
- **Character Reference**: Maintain consistent characters
- **Style Tuning**: Fine-tune visual aesthetics

## Performance

Midjourney v6 produces noticeably better results than v5.2, especially for human subjects and text rendering.

## Pricing

- **Basic**: $10/month (fast generation hours)
- **Standard**: $30/month (unlimited slow + more fast)
- **Mega**: $120/month (4x fast hours)
